# Privacy Policy for Mealio

**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** February 8, 2026

Mealio ("the App") is a meal diary application developed by Youngseok Joo ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

We believe in transparency and simplicity. This policy is written in plain language so you can understand exactly what happens with your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** When you sign in with Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL from the authentication provider. We do not receive or store your password.
- **Meal Entries:** Text notes, meal type, date and time, ratings, and "would eat again" preferences that you enter when logging meals.
- **Nutrition Information:** Calorie counts, macronutrient values (protein, fat, carbohydrates, fiber, sugar), and sodium amounts, whether entered manually or generated by AI analysis.
- **Ingredients:** Ingredient names and quantities you associate with meal entries.
- **Photos:** Meal photos you take with your camera or select from your photo library. Photos are uploaded to our servers only when you are signed in to an account.
- **Location Data (Optional):** If you choose to tag a meal with a location, we collect the location name, latitude, longitude, and address. Location access requires your explicit permission and can be denied or revoked at any time through your device settings.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Crash Reports and Diagnostics:** We use Sentry for error tracking. When the App crashes or encounters an error, Sentry collects technical information including device model, operating system version, app version, and crash stack traces. Sentry is configured with personally identifiable information (PII) collection disabled (`sendDefaultPii: false`), meaning it does not capture usernames, email addresses, or IP addresses in error reports.
- **Device Information:** Basic device type and operating system version included in crash reports.
- **Authentication Tokens:** We store hashed authentication tokens and optional device information (e.g., "iPhone 15") to manage your active sessions.

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** collect advertising identifiers.
- We do **not** serve advertisements.
- We do **not** use analytics tracking (such as PostHog or similar services) at this time.
- We do **not** access your camera or photo library without your explicit permission.
- We do **not** access your location without your explicit permission.

---

## 2. Guest Mode

Mealio offers a guest mode that works entirely offline. When using guest mode:

- All data (meal entries, photos, settings) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (MMKV).
- **No data is sent to our servers.**
- No account is created.
- No personal information is collected by us.

If you later create an account, locally stored guest data remains on your device unless you choose to sync it.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the Mealio service:

- **To provide the App's features:** Storing and displaying your meal entries, photos, nutrition data, ingredients, and location tags.
- **To authenticate you:** Verifying your identity when you sign in and managing your active sessions.
- **To provide AI meal analysis:** Processing your meal photos and entry data to generate nutritional estimates.
- **To maintain and improve the App:** Using crash reports and error diagnostics to identify and fix bugs.
- **To manage your account:** Processing account settings, preferences, and account deletion requests.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Mealio service.

---

## 4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

### 4.1 Infrastructure

When you use Mealio with an account, your data is processed and stored using the following services:

| Data Type | Service Provider | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Account data, meal entries, nutrition, locations, ingredients | Neon (PostgreSQL database) | Cloud-hosted |
| Meal photos | Cloudflare R2 (object storage) | Cloudflare global network |
| API server | Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run) | Cloud-hosted |
| Crash reports | Sentry | Sentry cloud infrastructure |

### 4.2 Security Measures

- Authentication tokens are hashed before storage.
- API communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
- Photo uploads use time-limited presigned URLs that expire automatically.
- Database access is restricted to the application server.
- Account deletion revokes all active authentication tokens.

### 4.3 Data Retention

- Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
- When you delete your account, your user record is soft-deleted (marked as deleted) and all authentication tokens are immediately revoked. You will no longer be able to access the account.
- Crash reports in Sentry are retained according to Sentry's standard retention policy (typically 90 days).

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Mealio. These services process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes:

- **Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In:** Authentication only. We receive your name, email, and profile photo URL. We do not share your Mealio data back with Apple or Google through these services.
- **Neon (Database):** Stores your account data, meal entries, and related content.
- **Cloudflare R2:** Stores your uploaded meal photos.
- **Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run):** Hosts our API server that processes your requests.
- **Sentry:** Receives crash reports and error diagnostics for application stability monitoring. PII collection is disabled.
- **Google Maps (API):** Used to display maps for location-tagged meals. Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing or advertising purposes.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 All Users

- **Camera and Photo Library Access:** You can grant or revoke permission at any time in your device Settings.
- **Location Access:** You can grant or revoke permission at any time in your device Settings. Location tagging is entirely optional.
- **Guest Mode:** You can use Mealio without creating an account. No data leaves your device.

### 6.2 Account Holders

- **Access Your Data:** You can view all your meal entries, photos, and account information within the App.
- **Update Your Data:** You can edit or delete individual meal entries, photos, and account information at any time.
- **Delete Your Account:** You can delete your account from the Settings screen within the App. This immediately revokes all sessions and marks your account as deleted.
- **Sign Out:** You can sign out at any time, which ends your current session.

### 6.3 European Union Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Legal Basis for Processing:** We process your data based on (a) your consent when you create an account and provide data, (b) the necessity to perform the service you requested, and (c) our legitimate interest in maintaining App stability through crash reporting.
- **Right of Access:** You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. You can do this directly through the account deletion feature in the App, or by contacting us.
- **Right to Data Portability:** You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Right to Object:** You have the right to object to processing of your personal data.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** You have the right to request restriction of processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

### 6.4 California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect about you.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Be free from discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
