# Account & Data Deletion

## How to Delete Your Mealio Account

To delete your account and all associated data:

1. Open the Mealio app
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **"Delete Account"**
4. Confirm deletion

## What Gets Deleted

- Your account and profile information
- All meal entries
- All photos
- Location data
- All personal data

Deletion is immediate and permanent. This action cannot be undone.
